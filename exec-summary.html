Where PuroClean Is Today

PuroClean has built a 25-year operational foundation — 500+ franchise locations, 24/7 emergency response, preferred provider for 3 of the top 5 national insurance carriers. The franchise model that made PuroClean great is also where the opportunity lives. Each of those 500 locations runs its own tech stack, tracks its own jobs, and manages its own customer relationships. Corporate can see about 20% of what's happening across the network. The other 80% operates in the dark.

Salesforce proposes a three-phase platform addressing all four gaps simultaneously — replacing fragile custom integrations with a governed API layer, giving the BizDev team a proper CRM, and unlocking real-time executive intelligence through Tableau. This is not a rip-and-replace. PuroClean's existing data lake, SPAR vendor model, and franchise autonomy all remain intact. Salesforce adds the intelligence layer on top.
